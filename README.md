New Game Concept: A Cinematic Single-Player Action RPG
I think this is a much stronger direction.

Let's change the philosophy of the project completely.

We are not building a multiplayer game, an MMORPG, a character-collection game or a large sandbox with hundreds of customization options.

We are building a story-driven, cinematic, single-player action RPG for Android and iOS, where the quality of the story, atmosphere, visuals, music, characters and combat matters more than the number of features.

Think of it as a console-style cinematic adventure adapted for mobile.

THE LAST LIGHT OF ASTER

1. GAME OVERVIEW
The Last Light of Aster is a story-driven, third-person cinematic action RPG set in a mysterious fantasy world slowly disappearing into darkness.

Players take control of Eren Vale, a former royal sentinel who has spent years trying to forget a tragedy from his childhood.

When an ancient bell rings in an abandoned village, Eren hears a voice that should no longer exist.

It belongs to his younger sister, Mara, who disappeared twelve years ago.

Nobody remembers her. Her name has disappeared from records, photographs and memories. Even Eren struggles to remember her face.

But he remembers her voice.

Following this mysterious signal takes Eren on a dangerous journey through abandoned kingdoms, forgotten temples, corrupted forests and the ruins of an ancient civilization.

What begins as a search for his sister gradually becomes a discovery about the truth behind the world's destruction.

Core experience:
- Experience an emotionally engaging fantasy story.
- Explore beautifully designed environments.
- Fight enemies through responsive third-person combat.
- Encounter memorable and challenging bosses.
- Discover secrets and forgotten histories.
- Experience cinematic cutscenes and character interactions.
- Uncover the truth behind the disappearance of an entire civilization.
The game prioritizes storytelling, visual presentation, atmosphere and combat quality over extensive customization or multiplayer systems.
2. THE WORLD AND ITS HISTORY
The World of Aster
Aster was once a prosperous world protected by an ancient source of light known as the First Dawn.

Its kingdoms were connected through ancient roads, enormous stone cities and temples built around mysterious energy sources.

People believed the light would never disappear.

But the First Dawn was not eternal.

Thousands of years ago, an ancient civilization discovered a mysterious dimension beyond the physical world. It became known as the Quiet.

The Quiet was not a place of ordinary darkness. It was a dimension where forgotten memories, lost identities and abandoned souls existed.

An attempt to harness its power caused a catastrophic event known as the Sundering.

A barrier called the Veil formed between the two worlds.

The Veil protected humanity, but it also separated the living from everything that had been lost.

For centuries, the ancient kingdoms maintained this barrier through a network of enormous structures called the Dawn Engines.

Eventually, the engines began to fail.
The First Dimming
Twelve years before the beginning of the game, the central Dawn Engine experienced a catastrophic failure.

The sky darkened.

Entire villages disappeared.

People began forgetting their families, their histories and even their own identities.

The royal authorities claimed that an unknown plague had destroyed the affected settlements.

The truth was far worse.

The kingdom had discovered that memories could be used to maintain the Veil.

The royal council secretly began sacrificing the memories of entire communities to prevent the barrier from collapsing.

One of those communities was Eren's hometown.

His younger sister Mara disappeared during the event.

The authorities erased every record of her existence.

Eren survived, but his memories became fragmented.

He grew up believing that something terrible had happened in his childhood, without understanding what it was.
3. MAIN STORY
Opening – The Village Without a Name
The game opens during a violent storm.

The camera moves across a ruined landscape, passing broken statues, abandoned houses and enormous towers disappearing into the clouds.

A narrator speaks:

"The world did not end in fire. It ended quietly. One name at a time."

The camera approaches an abandoned village.

Inside a ruined house, Eren wakes from a recurring nightmare.

He hears a bell.

It is coming from an abandoned tower outside the village.

Eren takes his sword and investigates.

Inside the tower, he discovers an ancient mechanism covered in unfamiliar symbols.

When he touches it, he experiences a vision of a little girl running through a field.

She turns around.

"Eren... you promised you would find me."

The vision disappears.

For the first time in twelve years, Eren remembers his sister's voice.

The game begins.
Chapter 1 – The Forgotten Village
Eren explores the abandoned village and discovers evidence that people once lived there.

He encounters corrupted creatures that appear to be protecting something.

After defeating them, he discovers an ancient memory fragment.

The fragment reveals a hidden road leading towards the old capital.

At the end of the chapter, Eren encounters a mysterious woman named Sera.

She recognizes the symbol on his sword and tells him that the kingdom has been hiding something for generations.

She agrees to guide him towards the capital.
Chapter 2 – The Road of Silence
Eren and Sera travel through an abandoned mountain settlement.

They discover that the corruption is spreading.

People who remain too close to the Veil gradually lose their memories and become hostile creatures known as the Hollowed.

Eren encounters a former royal soldier who recognizes him.

The soldier reveals that Eren was once part of a secret royal unit responsible for protecting the Dawn Engine.

Eren does not remember this.

The chapter ends with an encounter against the first major boss: The Warden of Stone.

After the battle, Eren discovers that his sword was once used to protect the Dawn Engine.
Chapter 3 – The Cathedral of Names
The journey leads to an enormous abandoned cathedral.

Its walls are covered with thousands of names.

Eren discovers that these are the names of people removed from the world's collective memory.

Among them is Mara.

Sera explains that the cathedral was built to preserve memories that the kingdom wanted forgotten.

Eren discovers a memory of the night his sister disappeared.

He sees himself as a child standing beside her while royal soldiers take her away.

Before he can understand what happened, the cathedral awakens.

A powerful creature known as the Keeper of Names emerges.

Eren defeats it and recovers another fragment.

The fragment reveals that Mara was not killed.

She was taken somewhere beyond the Veil.
Chapter 4 – The Glasswood
Eren enters a mysterious forest where memories become visible.

The trees contain fragments of people's lives.

He sees memories of his childhood, his parents and his sister.

Some memories are beautiful.

Others reveal that his family was connected to the royal council.

Eren discovers that his mother worked as a keeper of the Dawn Engine.

She knew what the kingdom was doing.

She tried to prevent Mara's disappearance but failed.

A major encounter takes place against the Glasswood Guardian.

After defeating it, Eren discovers that the Veil is not simply weakening.

Something on the other side is deliberately opening it.
Chapter 5 – The Fallen Capital
Eren reaches the remains of the royal capital.

The city is enormous, but almost completely abandoned.

Its streets are filled with statues of forgotten rulers, destroyed temples and remnants of the royal army.

He discovers the truth about the First Dimming.

The kingdom knowingly sacrificed entire communities to maintain the barrier.

The royal council believed this was necessary to save humanity.

Eren also learns that Sera was once a royal archivist.

She helped document the disappearances before the authorities destroyed the records.

At the heart of the capital, Eren encounters the Pale Regent.

The Regent reveals that Mara is still alive, but she has become part of the mechanism maintaining the Veil.

Eren refuses to accept this.

The Regent disappears, leaving the capital collapsing around him.
Chapter 6 – Beneath the Sunless Sea
Eren discovers an underground passage leading beneath an ancient sea.

This region contains the remains of the civilization that first discovered the Quiet.

The player explores enormous underground structures, flooded chambers and ancient laboratories.

Through recovered memories, Eren learns the truth about the Veil.

The Quiet was never an invading force.

It was a natural dimension containing the memories of everything that had been lost.

The Dawn Engines were built to prevent the natural passage between life and death.

By forcing the Veil to remain closed, the kingdom created the very corruption it feared.

The chapter ends with a confrontation against the Ancient Sentinel.

After defeating it, Eren discovers the location of the central Dawn Engine.
Chapter 7 – The Last Guardian
Eren reaches the central structure.

The Pale Regent finally reveals her identity.

She is Queen Maereth, the last ruler of Aster.

She explains that the Dawn Engine is consuming memories to maintain the remaining light.

Mara has become its living anchor.

If the Engine stops, the barrier will collapse.

If it continues, the entire world will eventually lose its identity.

The Queen believes that sacrificing a few generations is justified if humanity survives.

Eren realizes that his sister is not the source of the corruption.

She is its prisoner.

The chapter ends with an enormous battle against the Queen and the final royal guardians.

Eren defeats her.

Before dying, the Queen tells him:

"You cannot save a world that refuses to remember what it has done."
Chapter 8 – The Last Light
Eren enters the heart of the Dawn Engine.

Inside, the environment is unlike anything seen before.

Memories, broken buildings and fragments of the physical world float in an endless space.

He finally finds Mara.

She is older, but she recognizes him.

She explains that she has been holding the Veil together for twelve years.

She also reveals that their mother helped her survive by hiding a fragment of Eren's childhood memory inside his sword.

That is why Eren could still remember her voice.

Eren discovers that he has two apparent options:
- Leave the Engine running and allow the world to continue slowly forgetting.
- Destroy the Engine and risk releasing everything trapped beyond the Veil.
He chooses a third path.

He uses the ancient mechanism to release the stored memories instead of destroying them.

The Engine begins to collapse.

Across Aster, people begin remembering the names, faces and lives they had lost.

The creatures corrupted by the Quiet begin to disappear.

Mara's physical body cannot survive the collapse.

She and Eren share one final conversation.

Mara tells him:

"You kept your promise. You remembered me."

She disappears as the first natural sunrise in twelve years appears over the horizon.

Eren returns to the village.

He places his sword beside the old bell tower.

The camera slowly moves towards the sunrise.

The narrator speaks:

"Some things cannot be brought back. But nothing that is truly remembered is ever completely lost."

The End.
4. MAIN CHARACTERS
Eren Vale – The Protagonist
A former royal sentinel.

Eren is a quiet, determined warrior carrying the emotional weight of a childhood tragedy.

He is not a chosen king or a legendary hero.

He is an ordinary person who refuses to accept the disappearance of someone he loves.

Character development:
- Begins as a broken and emotionally distant soldier.
- Gradually discovers his forgotten history.
- Learns that the kingdom's greatest tragedy was deliberately hidden.
- Ultimately chooses truth and remembrance over preserving a false world.
Mara Vale – The Lost Sister
Mara is the emotional center of the story.

She disappeared during the First Dimming and became connected to the Dawn Engine.

Although she appears briefly through visions and memories, her presence drives the entire narrative.

She represents hope, innocence and the importance of remembering the past.
Sera – The Archivist
Sera is a former royal archivist.

She knows more about the kingdom than she initially reveals.

She serves as Eren's guide and provides historical context throughout the journey.

Her own guilt over helping conceal the disappearances creates an important character arc.
Queen Maereth – The Antagonist
The last ruler of Aster.

Maereth believes that maintaining the Veil is the only way to prevent humanity's extinction.

She is not motivated by greed or a desire for power.

She is a ruler who has sacrificed morality in the name of survival.

Her motivations make her a more interesting antagonist than a conventional evil ruler.
5. GAMEPLAY AND COMBAT
The gameplay is built around third-person melee combat and exploration.
Controls
- Virtual joystick for movement.
- Camera movement through touch.
- Basic sword attack.
- Heavy attack.
- Dodge.
- Special ability.
- Lock-on targeting.
- Contextual interaction.
Combat Philosophy
Combat should feel powerful, responsive and cinematic.

Enemies should have recognizable attack patterns.

Bosses should require timing, positioning and observation.

The player should feel the impact of every successful attack.
Player Progression
Keep customization minimal.

The player has one main character and one primary weapon.

Progression happens through story-based ability unlocks.

Three ability branches:
1. Steel: Stronger sword attacks and improved combos.
2. Resolve: Defensive abilities, counterattacks and stamina improvements.
3. Echo: Special abilities connected to the ancient power of the Veil.
No complicated equipment collection, character gacha or extensive cosmetic systems.
6. GAME MODES
Story Mode
The main game experience.

Players follow the complete narrative through eight chapters.
Chapter Replay
After completing a chapter, players can replay it to revisit important story moments and boss encounters.
Boss Remembrance
An optional mode allowing players to fight previously defeated bosses again.

This mode focuses on combat mastery and score-based challenges.
Difficulty Settings
- Story: More forgiving combat and accessible boss encounters.
- Standard: Intended default experience.
- Veteran: More demanding enemy damage, timing and combat challenges.
All story content remains available regardless of difficulty.

There will be no multiplayer, PvP, guilds or online competitive systems.
7. LEVEL AND WORLD STRUCTURE
The game uses handcrafted environments rather than a massive seamless open world.

Eight chapters form the main campaign.

Each chapter contains:
- One main environment.
- Several exploration areas.
- Story encounters.
- Combat encounters.
- Optional discoveries.
- One major narrative event.
- A chapter-ending objective or boss.
The player can explore surrounding areas but cannot freely travel anywhere in the world.

This allows the development team to spend more time improving visual quality, lighting, animations and environmental storytelling.
8. VISUAL AND AUDIO DIRECTION
The game must feel cinematic.
Visual style
- Realistic fantasy architecture.
- Detailed environments.
- Atmospheric fog and volumetric effects where supported.
- Dramatic lighting.
- Carefully directed camera movements.
- High-quality character animation.
- Detailed boss designs.
- Strong environmental composition.
Cinematic presentation
Cutscenes should be created using the actual game characters and environments.

Important story moments should use controlled camera framing, facial animation, environmental effects and music.

The player should experience a consistent visual style between gameplay and cinematics.
Music and sound
Create an original soundtrack.

Different regions should have their own musical identity.

Use orchestral arrangements, atmospheric soundscapes, subtle environmental audio and impactful combat sound effects.

Voice acting should be considered for the principal characters.

- One-time purchase to unlock the full story.
- No mandatory advertisements.
- No energy system.
- No pay-to-win purchases.
- No loot boxes.

CORE PRINCIPLE

The Last Light of Aster should be remembered as a beautiful and emotionally powerful journey.

It should not attempt to compete through the number of characters, weapons, modes or customization options.

Its competitive advantage should be the quality of its story, visual direction, sound, atmosphere and combat experience.

Important mobile graphics reality
I want to make one distinction: top-quality visuals do not necessarily mean using every expensive rendering feature.

Unreal Engine 5.8 supports mobile development, but its mobile rendering path has different feature and hardware limitations from desktop rendering. Epic specifically recommends designing graphics tiers and optimizing assets, lighting and materials for target devices. 

Epic Developer Community+2

My recommendation:
- Target 30 FPS as a stable baseline on supported devices.
- Offer 60 FPS on capable devices.
- Use carefully baked lighting and selective dynamic lighting.
- Make the environment visually rich through composition, materials and art direction.
- Use high-quality animation and cinematic camera work.
- Avoid making the entire world unnecessarily large.
I would rather have eight breathtaking environments than 50 mediocre ones.
